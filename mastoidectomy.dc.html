Ear — Procedure

Mastoidectomy

A surgical procedure used to remove an infected portion of the mastoid bone when conventional medical treatment has proven ineffective.

Overview

The mastoid bone sits behind the ear and consists of air spaces that help drain the middle ear. A mastoidectomy removes infected portions of this bone once conventional medical treatment has proven ineffective.
