About Dr Kelly

A specialist trusted by patients and referring doctors alike

After completing medical training at the University of Queensland, Dr Kelly undertook specialist training in Otolaryngology, Head and Neck Surgery and attained Fellowship of the Royal Australasian College of Surgeons in 1995.

She combines this wealth of experience with care and compassion to provide timely, effective management — believing that good communication with patients, relatives, referring doctors and allied health practitioners is integral to optimal patient care.
