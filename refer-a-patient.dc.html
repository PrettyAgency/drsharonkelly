Where Does Dr Kelly Operate?

Dr Kelly operates at Greenslopes Private Hospital, St Andrew's War Memorial Hospital Spring Hill, and Herston Private Hospital in Bowen Hills, for both adults and children — and at the Royal Brisbane and Women's Hospital for patients without private health insurance.

Paediatrics

These hospitals all offer excellent paediatric nursing care support, and Dr Kelly only uses specialist paediatric anaesthetists for your child's procedure — a dedicated, comprehensive team chosen for their ability to provide the highest level of care.
